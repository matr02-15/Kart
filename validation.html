Private validation

Replay a real recording and compare physical-line marks against independent crossings. Files stay in your browser.

Motion-pattern recognition is not proof of distance, exact position or official lap timing. Use a synchronised reference at the same physical line.

Independent timing evidence

Reference file: crossing_s header, followed by crossing times in seconds, one per line. Include exactly the same recording interval; timestamps must be independently aligned to the recording clock.

Automatic virtual-point crossings are shown in the replay, but are not substituted for physical-line marks.
